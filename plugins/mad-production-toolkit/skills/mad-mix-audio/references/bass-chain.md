# Optional bass chain

Use this when the cover bass lacks weight or consistency after timing is correct. Make each change level matched and listen in the complete mix, especially where the kick, rhythm instruments, and vocal overlap.

1. Compare the cover bass and any authorized guide at the same section and similar perceived loudness. Note whether the issue is sub energy, note-to-note dynamics, midrange audibility, or masking. Do not infer a needed EQ curve from one waveform peak.
2. High-pass only as needed for unwanted rumble. Adjust broad low and low-mid bands for the arrangement. A small gain change can be better than a new effect when the recording is already balanced.
3. Apply a compressor when the performance has excess dynamic spread. Choose threshold, ratio, attack, release, knee, and makeup gain by ear and document them in the mix config or DAW project. Check that note attacks remain intact.
4. If a modeled amp/cab is desired, choose a NAM capture and cabinet IR you can legally use. Render that chain outside this toolkit, compensate latency, and export a wet stem. Add it to the same bass bus as the dry stem with explicit level automation. Compare dry/wet and bypass at matched loudness.
5. Recheck full-band headroom, mono compatibility, low-frequency build-up, and final loudness. Keep the model/IR license and hash in private project notes; do not publish those binaries as part of this skill.

No particular amplifier, cabinet, model, frequency, ratio, or gain is a toolkit default.
